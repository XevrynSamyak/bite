BITE is a social food-discovery app that predicts which restaurants you'll love from your taste, your friends' experiences and where people like you keep going back.

Ratings tell you what strangers think. BITE answers a better question: "Which restaurant will I probably love?"

BITE is in development. We're building the first version for one city, and the waitlist is open. Join it.

How it works

  1. Set your taste. Cuisines, veg, non-veg or vegan, budget per meal, casual or fine dining, street food or premium, cafés and desserts.
  2. Follow food friends. See the visits they choose to share, within their privacy settings.
  3. Eat and log. BITE learns from what you save, skip, visit and revisit.
  4. Get your picks. Every place is scored on seven signals: your taste, friends' visits, repeat visits, budget fit, distance, cuisine preferences and recent experiences.

    What BITE knows about you

    BITE learns from

    • Places you save and visits you log
    • Reviews you write and friends you follow
    • Places you go back to
    • Your preferences and budget
    • Reservations made through BITE

    BITE won't

    • Read bank or UPI data without your explicit consent
    • Show your visits to anyone you haven't allowed
    • Let restaurants pay to change your picks

    A repeat visit is a signal, not proof: people also go back because a place is close, cheap or convenient, so BITE weighs it alongside everything else.

    Questions

    Is BITE free?

    Yes. A premium tier with deeper personalisation, exclusive collections and planning tools is planned.

    Where does restaurant information come from?

    Reputable data providers, licensed datasets and restaurant partnerships, starting with a hand-picked set of places in the launch city.

    I run a restaurant. How do I join?

    Join the waitlist to hear when partnerships open. Partners get reservations, labelled promotions and aggregated customer insights.

    What's the long-term plan?

    A personal food graph that connects people, cuisines, restaurants, budgets and experiences. We start social-first and add AI personalisation as real usage grows.
